candidate and keep full diagnostics. If it fails,evidence remains and full mode
remains the baseline. If two cores measurably help native execution,prepare a
Linux capacity qualification next;otherwise investigate per-core execution cost.
Do not claim a Wine penalty from the existing cross-hardware Windows/Linux data.

no server-caused publication gap above100ms,at least three alternating paired
five-minute runs per tested load,defined RTT/jitter,concurrency/resource/startup
checks and later player acceptance. Refer to linux-native-coordinator-acceptance.txt.
Local implementation/testing is authorized. Live deployment,installer/release
replacement,host resizing and new cloud spending are separate later actions.
