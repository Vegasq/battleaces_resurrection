match needs qualified server-side bot execution or explicit single-owner AI
delegation with authenticated assignment,input checks,ordered frames and owner-
loss behavior. Independent local fallback worlds for each human are invalid.

production, movement, combat and results. See server-ai-runtime.txt. Actual worker
