The existing community1/public1 packets retain their exact old shapes. TLS and

  inserted after its eight units and before ready/returned flags. Empty lobby
  snapshots also retain this explicit version prefix. Maximum two Custom humans.

Selection:32 raw catalog SHA256 bytes,then exactly17 [slot:u32,item:u32] pairs.
All integers are little endian. Slot IDs are positive/distinct;nonzero item IDs
are distinct. Zero is reserved for genuinely empty installed slots. Complete
selections require exact native slot order and matching installed compatibility
matrix/catalog before storage or match mutation. Unknown/wrong-slot items fail.
No caller-supplied entitlement,portrait or banner fields are trusted;the server
rebuilds these from the validated selection and installed role configuration.

SET_LOADOUT atomically saves units and full selection before updating session
state or advertising full-protocol capability. Invalid catalogs/items and failed
file replacement retain prior file/session/capability state and return a negative
ACK. Old SET_DECK51 preserves complete cosmetics and does not downgrade a full
session. LOBBY68 is sent only to sessions that successfully published a complete
selection or entered through66;other recipients retain LOBBY44.

ENTER66 validates and freezes its own complete intent before waiting/proposals.
The resulting Entry and Room retain the full-protocol assignment decision even
if a later session/disk value changes. Each full participant receives MATCH67
with that exact frozen selection;legacy entries receive MATCH63. Bot selections,
frame64,READY60,CANCEL59,RETURN61 and shared ACK53 retain existing formats.

The client freezes a fresh native human deck/17-slot selection at Play and Custom
Start/Ready intent. Background publication and Custom operations correlate both
request and command kind;an older ACK cannot accept a newer selection. Custom
start handlers also receive their own publication ACK rather than having it
consumed by a background handler. Member comparison includes catalog and all slots.
Host edits before dispatch require a new Start;guest edits racing a start cancel
Ready. Accepted joins use the owned frozen human deck rather than re-reading a
later mutable picker/ProfileCache. Public assignment checks bind the same complete
snapshot to ticket,connection generation,mode and proposal. Local fallback receives
the original frozen intent too;it must not reread later selections after waiting.
