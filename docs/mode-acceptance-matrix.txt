Purpose and closure rule
This is the finite inventory for D2, not another roadmap or a new feature list.
PASS below always names its scope. Retained automated native passes do not mean
physical input, protected enrollment, LAN, final artifacts or all visible UI
have passed. Reuse unchanged applicable evidence. A changed implementation needs
affected checks, not a blanket rerun of every historical case.
OPEN cases may close through inspected existing evidence if it proves the claim;
otherwise run the specified case, or fix and retest its concrete failure.
Do not add scenarios indefinitely: additions require an identified uncovered D2
requirement or reproducible defect. Other delivery gates stay in their own items.
